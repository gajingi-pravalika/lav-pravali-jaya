Major Challenges Caused by El Niño

☀️ Water Shortage

Delayed monsoons lead to depleted groundwater levels. Water tables in ponds and borewells drop significantly.

🌱 Pest Outbreaks

High temperatures increase the incidence of sap-sucking pests and severe crop infestations.

🌾 Paddy Crop Damage

Water-intensive crops like rice/paddy face a high risk of drying up and fungal blast attack.

Water Management Practices

💧 Drip Irrigation

Delivers water directly to the roots, saving up to 50% more water compared to traditional methods.

🌾 Mulching

Covering soil around plant bases with dry straw or plastic sheets prevents moisture evaporation.

⏳ Short-Duration Varieties

Opt for short-duration seed varieties that reach harvest maturity within 90 to 100 days.

About Us

Empowering farmers with smart agricultural solutions during dry weather & El Niño conditions.

🎯 Our Mission

To support rural and small-scale farmers by offering timely, accurate, and easy-to-understand advice on crop selection, pest control, and water conservation during critical weather changes.

👁️ Our Vision

To build a climate-resilient farming community capable of sustaining high crop yield and income using modern, sustainable, and eco-friendly agricultural practices.

🤝 Community First

Providing 100% free agricultural guidance, chemical safety dosage recommendations, and direct support in local languages through Rythu Bharosa integration.

🧪 Expert Pesticide Guidance

Delivering accurate chemical name suggestions and dosages to stop crop loss from dry-weather pests.

Connecting farmers with agricultural specialists to address urgent field queries free of charge.
